Privacy policy

Ahead: What's That Landmark for Garmin watches

Ahead does not collect or store any data on a server of its own. The developer does not receive your position or any other data.

Data sent to third-party services

To find places, the watch sends your approximate position, the search distance and the selected categories through the paired phone (Garmin Connect) to the public Photon search service by komoot (photon.komoot.io). The service receives the request and your phone's IP address and handles them according to its own privacy policy.

When you open a place's link on the detail page, your phone opens its page on openstreetmap.org.

Data stored on the watch

The last search (the places found, where and when) is stored only on the watch, so that the places appear at once when you open Ahead again nearby. Your settings and category choices are stored on the watch and in the Connect IQ app on your phone.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.
