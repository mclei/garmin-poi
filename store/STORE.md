## App name

Ahead: What's That Landmark

(On the watch the app is called just "Ahead"; "Ahead" alone is hard to find in the store,
hence the descriptor.)

## Short description / tagline

Point your wrist at a building, castle or viewpoint and see what it is and how far away.

## Category

Watch App, store category **Navigation** (or Travel, if offered)

## Description

What's that place I'm looking at? Ahead names the landmark in front of you. Point your wrist,
and the watch shows the place you are facing with its name, distance and type. Turn around and
it updates to whatever you face now.

WHAT YOU SEE
• The place in front of you, large, with a direction arrow that turns green when you point
  straight at it.
• Nearby places as coloured dots on a compass ring.
• A detail page with the type, distance and bearing, street address and coordinates, and a
  link that opens the place on your phone.
• A list of everything nearby, sorted by distance, ahead of you or all around (360°).
• A glance that shows the place in front of you right from the watch face.

PLACES
Viewpoints and attractions, parks and nature, monuments and memorials, castles and forts,
ruins and archaeological sites, places of worship, museums and galleries, theatres and
cinemas, restaurants, cafés and bars. Turn each category on or off on the watch or in the
phone settings. A one-off filter shows just one category (for example only restaurants)
without changing your settings.

SMART RANGE
In a city Ahead looks only a few dozen metres around you; in the countryside the search
widens up to 5 km until it finds enough places.

LOCK A TARGET
Pick a place from the list and the arrow keeps pointing at it as you walk, until you clear
the lock.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Garmin watch with GPS and a compass (magnetometer).
• A paired phone with Garmin Connect and an internet connection. Places are looked up online.

## How to use (short guide for the description or the support page)

1. Start Ahead outdoors and wait for GPS ("Acquiring GPS"), or tap "Use last known" to start
   right away from your last position.
2. Point the watch where you are looking. The place in front of you is shown large.
3. Tap the screen (or press Start) for the detail page. Start on the detail page locks the
   place as your target.
4. Swipe up for the list of nearby places, swipe down for a one-off category filter, and
   swipe in from the right edge for the menu (categories, refresh, compass calibration).

## FAQ (support page)

**The arrow points the wrong way.**
Calibrate the compass: menu → Calibrate compass, then draw a figure eight with your arm. If it
stays wrong, calibrate the compass in the watch settings (Sensors → Compass), away from metal.

**It says "no phone" or shows an error.**
Ahead needs a connection to your phone with Garmin Connect running and internet access.

**I see nothing nearby.**
Check that some categories are turned on (menu → Filters). In remote places there may simply
be no mapped places within 5 km.

**Where does the data come from?**
From OpenStreetMap, the free map edited by volunteers. If a place is missing or wrong, you can
fix it on openstreetmap.org.

## Privacy

Ahead does not collect or store any data on a server of its own. To find places, the watch
sends your approximate position and the selected categories (through the paired phone) to the
public Photon search service by komoot (photon.komoot.io). The developer does not receive
your position. Category settings are stored only on the watch and in your Garmin Connect app.
